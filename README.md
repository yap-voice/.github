### What Yap does

Talk the way you normally do, in Hinglish, English or your own language. Yap turns it into clean, ready-to-send text in any app.

- 🫧 **Works everywhere.** A small bubble sits beside any text box: WhatsApp, Gmail, LinkedIn, anywhere you type.
- ✨ **Cleans up as you speak.** Punctuation, paragraphs and lists added, ums and repeats removed.
- 🌐 **Speaks your languages.** Hinglish, English, Hindi, Marathi and more, and it can write in a different language than the one you spoke.
- ✏️ **Edits by voice.** Select text and say what to change.
- 🔊 **Reads aloud.** Listen to any text with natural voices.
